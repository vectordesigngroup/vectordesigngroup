# Hi, I'm Daniel

I design and build software and websites in Gainesville, Florida. Vector Design Group is the label I put my design and build projects under. Most of what I make is for small businesses: tools that save time on real jobs, and sites that are simple to use.

## Projects

- **[FenceAI](https://fenceai.app)**: Quoting software for fence contractors. Trace the fence on a satellite map, build the material list from your own pricebook, and send a signed quote from the yard. Built by FenceAI LLC.
- **[Gainesville Backyards](https://gainesvillebackyards.com)**: My fence and backyard company in Gainesville, FL (Gainesville Backyards LLC). Fences, hardscaping, pergolas, pressure washing and more.
- **[Bitcoin Basics](https://bitcoinbasicsbook.com)**: A book on understanding the future of money through quotes.
- **[Bitcoin Basics Network](https://bitcoin-basics-network.vercel.app)**: A live dashboard of Bitcoin network stats that goes with the book.
- **[Gainesville Padel Club](https://gainesvillepadel.club)**: A padel club community in Gainesville.
- **[BitPadel](https://github.com/vectordesigngroup/bitpadel)**: An open-source, Bitcoin-native "club in a box" for padel clubs: booking, memberships, Lightning and card payments, and NFC door access. Early prototype (AGPL-3.0).
- **[Macanao Cacao](https://vectordesigngroup.github.io/macanao-cacao-site/)**: Pre-launch site for Venezuelan fine-flavor cacao from Margarita Island.
